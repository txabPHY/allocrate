what is IDEngine?

Snap or paste a photo of a tracklist — a festival flyer, a screen, a scribbled note — and IDEngine reads the text, splits it into artist and song, and gives you one-tap search links for YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and Spotify. Everything happens in your browser; no image is uploaded anywhere.
